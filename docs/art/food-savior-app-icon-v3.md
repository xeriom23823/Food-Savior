## 完整 prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand, app icon refinement.
Create a single finished square Food Savior app icon.
Reference image 1 is the current app icon: preserve its muted green full-bleed background and protective food-saving message, but replace the abstract apple symbol with a character.
Reference image 2 is the old Chihuahua mascot: keep Chihuahua identity (very large upright ears, rounded apple head, tiny short muzzle); completely reinterpret in a simpler warm picture-book style.
User request: incorporate a Chihuahua and the image of a Savior, with some of the warm humorous Japanese picture-book feeling of Bread Thief (パンどろぼう / 麵包小偷).
Subject: one original endearing cream-and-tan Chihuahua food-rescue hero, shown front-facing in a compact bust portrait with oversized ears, wearing a small warm golden hero cape that peeks clearly out behind its shoulders. Both little paws carefully hug a single plump golden loaf of bread against its chest, like rescuing something precious. A gently determined slightly goofy expression: small dark eyes, assertive little eyebrows, black button nose, tiny closed mouth. Very clearly a Chihuahua dog, not a cat or fox. Bread is ordinary bread, no face, no costume. Keep hero cape obvious but simple. Do not add letters or shield emblems.
Style: warm playful Japanese children's picture-book illustration; confident slightly wobbly thick dark brown outlines, tactile lightly grainy gouache fills, handmade asymmetry, charming understated deadpan humor. An original character design. Simple chunky shapes, almost no interior detail, easy recognition at app icon size.
Palette: muted green background near #3E8E5A; warm ivory dog, caramel ear patches and bread crust, warm golden cape, dark cocoa outline. Distinct silhouette against green.
Layout: only one central character and bread, tight unified silhouette entirely within central 72% square so ears and cape survive launcher masks. Comfortable equal margins. Entire square background is opaque muted green reaching all four edges. No baked rounded corners.
Avoid: text, logos, watermarks, multiple options, mockup, frame, photorealism, 3D, shiny anime eyes, detailed fur, tiny accessories, extra foods, scene backgrounds, dramatic action effects.
Output one square high-quality raster app icon.
```
